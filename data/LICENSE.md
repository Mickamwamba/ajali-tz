# Data licence

The datasets compiled by this project in `data/processed/` and `data/reference/` (the cleaned,
combined and derived figures, and the news crash records) are licensed under
**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

You may share and adapt them for any purpose, including commercially, as long as you give credit:
"Ajali TZ", plus the original source named in each row.

## Third-party material keeps its own terms

- **Police / NBS figures** and the files in `data/raw/nbs/` and `data/raw/census/`: published by the
  Tanzania Police Force and the National Bureau of Statistics. Cite them as the source of the numbers.
- **WHO estimates** (`data/raw/who/`): World Health Organization; see WHO's terms of use.
- **Boundaries** (`data/reference/tz_*.geojson`, `tz_districts.csv`): NBS / UN OCHA, CC BY-IGO.
- **World Bank population**: CC BY 4.0, World Bank.
- **News**: headlines and links in `data/news/` and `data/processed/news_crashes.*` belong to the
  outlets that published them (The Citizen, Mwananchi, Daily News, HabariLeo, The Chanzo). This
  project stores only facts and links, never article text.

The code is licensed separately under the MIT licence (see `LICENSE` in the repository root).
